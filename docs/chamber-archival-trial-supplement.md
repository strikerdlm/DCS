# Archival chamber and flight trials, release 1.19

Release 1.19 adds 10 source records and 34 observations. Two existing source records now link to verified full text. The search ledger contains 19 operations and 84 screened results. [Sources](../data/chamber/v1.19/sources.csv) · [Observations](../data/chamber/v1.19/observations.csv) · [Source updates](../data/chamber/v1.19/schema.sql) · [Search ledger](../data/chamber/v1.19/search_runs.csv)

- **Bridge et al., 1944.** In 167 chamber runs by 80 men at 38,000 ft, **104 runs had joint pain** at or above the source's reporting threshold, **56 had chokes of any source grade**, and **85 ended in early descent for any cause**. Table III reports **20 moderate or severe chokes** and **50 runs with both joint pain and chokes of any grade**. The postflight analysis includes 120 returned questionnaires. Of those, **19 reported symptoms in joints affected at altitude**. The exposure-level denominator and the follow-up denominator are recorded separately. [Original pp. 316–327](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/joam/15/5/article-p316.pdf)
- **Cheetham, South Africa, 1947.** **16/83 runs** and **11/28 officers** had joint symptoms. Table VII records 14 mild, four moderate, and three major source-defined symptom runs. The chamber start was 4,700 ft and the target pressure altitude was 37,000 ft. One officer was rejected after two runs. The report prescribes three hours and describes a two-hour period in one case. Actual individual exposure durations remain unavailable. [Original Tables I and VII](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/joam/18/3/article-p279.pdf)
- **Allen and Beard, 1969.** **45/102 man-flights** had bends across four cabin-to-suit profiles. Table III has distinct risk sets at 5 and 3.5 psia. The original paper confirms partial overlap with the 1971 body-fat publication. The oxygen summary denominator of 58 matches the original 32 plus 26 flights. The mixed-gas denominators and analyzed durations differ. [Original Tables II–VI](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/aeme/40/12/article-p1327.pdf) · [1971 body-fat table](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/aeme/42/5/article-p518.pdf#page=3)
- **McIver et al., 1967.** The report gives **15 symptomatic participants across 18 initial EVAs**, including some paresthesia-only responses. Five of the eight initially symptomatic participants with paired post-treatment scores had symptoms again during the second simulated EVA. Two severe cases match the prior detailed 1966 case series by explicit cross-reference. Their case reports contribute provenance. [1967 Tables I–II](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/aeme/38/10/article-p1034.pdf) · [1966 case series](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/aeme/37/12/article-p1266.pdf)
- **Meader, 1967.** The original resolves an earlier bibliographic lead and documents **36 episodes across 958 WU-2 flights**. The flight denominator pertains to the 11 crewmembers selected because they had at least one reported episode. The squadron-wide exposure denominator is unavailable. [Original Methods and Results](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/aeme/38/3/article-p301.pdf)
