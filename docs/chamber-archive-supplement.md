# Original journal archives and chamber outcome tables

Release 1.27 adds 45 observation rows and 37 published percentage rows from eight source records. It upgrades the existing Zhang citation and links the original 1957 paper to the previously indexed 1961 Marotta series. The audit trail records 31 search and retrieval operations and 83 selected title screenings or targeted source checks. The combined PubMed index screen covered 258 distinct titles. [Source registry](../data/chamber/v1.27/sources.csv) · [Observations](../data/chamber/v1.27/observations.csv) · [Published percentages](../data/chamber/v1.27/reported_rates.csv) · [Cohort links](../data/chamber/v1.27/overlap.csv) · [Search operations](../data/chamber/v1.27/search_runs.csv) · [Search hits](../data/chamber/v1.27/search_hits.csv)

| Original publication | Data entered and limits |
| --- | --- |
| Marbarger et al., 1957 | Table I reports six conditions in the same 33 men. Two-hour bends counts were 16 after direct ascent, then 2, 2, 5, 3 and 7 following 120 minutes of oxygen at ground level or at 8,000, 12,000, 18,000 or 22,000 ft. The two hour-specific columns partition each cumulative count. Two cells print 3.33% for one case among 33, which corresponds to about 3.03%. Both published formats are preserved. The trial protocols differ from Marotta 1961. Participant linkage and exact duplication with historical NASA data remain unresolved. [Marbarger Table I](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/joam/28/2/article-p127.pdf#page=5) |
| Rhodes, 1970 | Among 4,400 trainees and other flight personnel, none reported bends above the source's historical Grade I. The overall bends rate is reported as below 0.5%, and the exact all-grade count is unavailable. Ascertainment was by participant report after the chamber run. Overlap with broader Navy surveillance remains unresolved. [Rhodes Results and Table I](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/aeme/41/10/article-p1173.pdf#page=2) |
| Lawrence et al., 1945 | Among participants selected by prior chamber performance, 1/21 in the resistant group and 27/34 in the susceptible group were incapacitated in at least one of two **chamber** validation runs. The separate **aircraft** analysis recorded 1/13 and 12/16 incapacitations among completed qualifying flights. Duty crew reported 10 bends cases in 72 crew-flights, including four cases in 42 pilot-flights. [Lawrence Tables I–II and crew Results](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/joam/16/5/article-p286.pdf) |
| Gray et al., 1947 | Table I gives exact participant counts by number of forced descents after two to five chamber runs. The endpoint includes bends, chokes, gas pain and toothache. The four reported denominators are 285, 102, 50 and 43. Table IV reverses the test order and counts the same 40 cadets twice for its comparison. That table contributes zero additional observations. [Gray Methods and Table I](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/joam/18/1/article-p88.pdf#page=3) |
| Pilmanis et al., 2005 | Four test profiles give exact denominators and published cumulative percentages for clinical DCS, any VGE and Grade IV VGE. The experiment reports 139 exposures in 55 people. The database retains A–C historical AFRL comparator rates with overlap flags. It preserves Table II's 50% DCS for the Test C comparator alongside the abstract's 53% discrepancy in the source note. Test D's 31% comparator is an ADRAC model output and has no observed comparator row. [Pilmanis Tables I–II](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/asem/76/7/article-p635.pdf#page=3) |
| Parsons, 1959, and Katuntsev et al., 2001 | Parsons reports significant spirometry declines in 3 of 30 bends records, a case-conditional measurement. Katuntsev reports zero clinical signs in 24 ascents among six volunteers, with an unresolved discrepancy between that total and the abstract's description of paired exposures. Neither source supplies an independent risk estimate for routine training. [Parsons pp. 821–822](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/joam/30/11/article-p821.pdf) · [Katuntsev author abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11385985/) |

The 1999 Zhang abstract confirms 43 participants and describes age and prebreathe comparisons. Exact event counts and arm sizes remain unavailable. Henry's original 1956 paper supplies a cross-reference to the Donner Laboratory chamber data. Its fitted curves and rates provide no newly independent exposure tallies. The originating flight identifier for the 40-person AFRL staged ascent example from release 1.26 remains unresolved. [Zhang author abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11766705/) · [Henry publisher PDF](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/joam/27/3/article-p250.pdf) · [AFRL staged-ascent example](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.01137#page=16)
