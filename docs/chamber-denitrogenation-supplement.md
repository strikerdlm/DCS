## Original source checks

- **Taubman et al., Brazil, 2006.** Across 65 Brazilian Air Force chamber flights, **223/848 aircrew reported at least one symptom** during training. Nine of the 223 reported joint pain. The questionnaire could capture hypoxia and pressure-related symptoms, and the paper provides no adjudicated clinical DCS endpoint. The helicopter group has **23/106** in Results and **108** participants in Methods. The reported 24-hour findings lack a response denominator. Firecrawl returned the full text, including the Methods and Results. The source hash identifies the extracted Markdown. [Revista UNIFA, pp. 18–21](https://www2.fab.mil.br/unifa/images/revista/pdf/ed_21.pdf#page=18)
- **Allen et al., 1971.** Table I lines 2–14 give 26 body-fat-stratified percentages covering 883 person-exposures in 147 men. Several strata cite earlier studies, including Beard and Degner, which are already represented in the database. The external student-diver group in Table I line 1 and the historical comparisons in Table II remain outside these 883 exposures. The Results give **1/130** and **5/383**, described after discussing four-hour denitrogenation. The identifiable four-hour strata have **67** and **309** exposures. Adding the three-hour mixed-gas strata reproduces the two published denominators, so the exact prose counts retain an unresolved protocol and denominator flag. [Table I and Results, pp. 520–521](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/aeme/42/5/article-p518.pdf#page=3)
- **Waligora et al., NASA TM-58259.** Series 1–3 map to NEDU Appendix E and NASA Table F1 Tests 1–3. For Series 2 after 210 minutes of oxygen, the original Results and Table 5 give **7/22** symptomatic exposures and **14/22** with Doppler bubbles. Figure 15 gives **23** for that arm's mean bubble-grade curve. The report-wide total of 173 also implies one more exposure than the three series totals in Methods. NEDU and NASA 2019 give **7/23** for DCS. NASA 2019 gives **15/23** for any bubbles. The reason for the sample difference is unresolved. The original Table 5 also supplies paired symptom and Doppler endpoints. Five other matching any-VGE counts already appear in NASA Table F1. [1984 NASA report](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19840020323/downloads/19840020323.pdf) · [NASA 2019 Table F1](../data/chamber/v1/nasa_f1.csv) · [NEDU Appendix E](../data/chamber/v1/nedu_appendix_e.csv)
- **Cherniakov et al., 1976.** The public author abstract reports **0/6 participants** with decompression symptoms in a repeated-excursion chamber experiment. The six people took part in six three-day experiments. The report mentions seven excursions and gives two exposure configurations. Participant-by-excursion denominators and delayed follow-up are unavailable. The study also appears as reference 16 in the NASA legacy compilation. [Author abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/966673/) · [NASA legacy compilation](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/conkin-dcs-estimation-tp210196-2001.pdf)
- **Barer et al., 1979.** The author abstract describes 550 pressure-chamber tests in 200 suited people, with matching features to Genin’s suited comparison series. The abstract lacks arm-specific case counts and denominators. The relationship is recorded for deduplication. [Author abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36501/) · [Genin’s Table 2, PDF p. 10](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19800013491/downloads/19800013491.pdf#page=10)
