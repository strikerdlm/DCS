# Ferris and Engel neurological outcomes

The v1.62 evidence supplement extracts seven historical episode margins from Ferris and Engel’s 1951 chapter and five timeline milestones for one Berkeley episode reported in the June 1944 meeting minutes. Both databases retain citations to the report that supplied each value. [Clinical chapter, printed pp. 41–44 (PDF pp. 56–59)](https://archive.org/download/in.ernet.dli.2015.116475/2015.116475.Deoompression-Sickness-Caisson-Sickness-Divers-And-Fliers-Bends-And-Related-Syndeomes.pdf#page=56) · [1944 minutes, item 9 (PDF p. 14)](https://digirepo.nlm.nih.gov/ext/mm/101085500X796/PDF/101085500X796.pdf#page=14)

| Cincinnati | 32 within 30 minutes after descent, six completed the scotoma course at 35,000 ft without descent, and eight began at altitude and were unmodified by descent. The extracted denominators count syndrome episodes. Exposure denominators with uniform follow-up are unavailable for these margins. They remain case-conditional and outside the incidence model. [Cincinnati presentation, PDF p. 8](https://digirepo.nlm.nih.gov/ext/mm/101085500X234/PDF/101085500X234.pdf#page=8) · [Chapter pp. 43–44](https://archive.org/download/in.ernet.dli.2015.116475/2015.116475.Deoompression-Sickness-Caisson-Sickness-Divers-And-Fliers-Bends-And-Related-Syndeomes.pdf#page=58) · [Bibliography reference 509](https://archive.org/download/in.ernet.dli.2015.116475/2015.116475.Deoompression-Sickness-Caisson-Sickness-Divers-And-Fliers-Bends-And-Related-Syndeomes.pdf#page=469)

The two Berkeley narratives agree on age, the sequence of visual symptoms, the right-sided neurological findings, the cerebrospinal fluid protein result, and the partial visual recovery documented at 48 hours. The chapter places initial bends and chokes 18 minutes into the 38,000-ft exposure and scotoma onset during descent. The meeting minutes place worsening about 90 minutes after descent and the chapter gives approximately 105 minutes. Both remain source-specific milestones. The historical catalog now indexes the meeting case once. Chapter reference 383 identifies CAM Report 396. The 1946 journal article and 1945 Navy bulletin remain leads for full-text comparison.
