# Historical chamber outcomes and cohort reconciliation

Release 1.31 adds ten count rows and four source records. It supersedes three Houston rows that reused a cohort already entered in v1.24. Both Houston citations, the journal's incapacitating-bends margin and all frozen v1.30 files remain available. [Sources](../data/chamber/v1.31/sources.csv) · [New counts](../data/chamber/v1.31/observations.csv) · [Cohort links](../data/chamber/v1.31/overlap.csv) · [Correction and retained row IDs](../data/chamber/v1.31/schema.sql)

- **Griffin et al., 1946.** The original chamber table classifies **183 evaluable person-runs** at 35,000 ft as 131 with joint bends, 16 with respiratory distress labeled chokes, seven with nausea and 29 symptom-free through three hours. The paper excludes runs terminated early for another participant's removal. All 18 men had joint bends at least once after four to 35 trials each. The report gives only symptom scores by experimental condition, so the outcome counts remain pooled across conditions and held for cohort linkage to CAM Report 174. [Original Table II and clinical narrative](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/joam/17/1/article-p56.pdf#page=9)
- **Browne et al., 1966.** Two of four selected participants developed mild symptoms the authors attributed to aeroembolism during repeat capsule tests. Both cases occurred during the three-hour condition at 194 mm Hg total pressure. The methods prescribe two hours of preoxygenation, while the case account describes one hour initially and approximately 1.5 hours after a regulator change. The exact number of runs by oxygen-delivery condition remains unverified. [NASA report, p. 5-27](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19680006397/downloads/19680006397.pdf#page=68)
- **Houston, Pensacola cohort.** The 1946 Navy report and 1947 paper describe the same **387 lock ascents, 20 operating staff, six affected people, three scotomas and two persistent visual losses**. The original Navy outcome margins are retained. The 1947 conditional visual-loss fraction, 2/3, remains in the supersession record as an alternate denominator for the same two people. The unique journal margin of two staff with incapacitating bends remains available as an overlapping outcome. [Navy report](https://archive.org/download/DTIC_AD0622164/DTIC_AD0622164.pdf#page=4) · [Journal report](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/joam/18/2/article-p165.pdf)
- **Conkin et al., 1992 HDSD.** The full article's records 325–328 match the sizes and bends percentages of the four Maxwell Field groups in Motley. The release links the repeated cohorts. The remaining anonymous rows lack a verified source-to-cohort mapping. [HDSD Table III](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/asem/63/9/article-p819.pdf#page=3)
