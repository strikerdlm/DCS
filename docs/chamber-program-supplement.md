# Chamber programs and historical case reports, release 1.21

Release 1.21 adds 32 observation rows, 10 source records and 14 cohort links. The search ledger contains 32 operations and 142 logged results or retrievals. The observations include chamber outcome counts, margins for different endpoints in the same exposures, and individual case reports with no underlying exposure denominator. [Observations](../data/chamber/v1.21/observations.csv) · [Sources](../data/chamber/v1.21/sources.csv) · [Cohort links](../data/chamber/v1.21/overlap.csv) · [Search operations](../data/chamber/v1.21/search_runs.csv) · [Search hits](../data/chamber/v1.21/search_hits.csv)

- **Katuntsev, Russia, 1996.** The author's dissertation summary reports symptomatic DCS in **72/106** elevated-CO₂ exposures and **33/101** oxygen control exposures across three pressures. The 1994 SAE abstract independently verifies the transcription of both counts from the same research program. The summary also reports **19/78** symptomatic exposures at 38 kPa in the head-down arm and **17/58** at 40 kPa in the upright arm. Doppler bubble findings for those arms were **46/78** and **44/58**. At 9,000 m, Table 2 gives **30/38** and **9/20** symptomatic ascents for the oxygen prebreathe rest and exercise groups. Pressure, posture and exposure-level denominators are preserved as reported. [Author summary](https://medical-diss.com/medicina/vysotnaya-dekompressionnaya-bolezn-eksperimentalnoe-issledovanie-patogeneza-i-putey-profilaktiki) · [SAE primary abstract](https://doi.org/10.4271/941332)
- **Skedina, Russia, 1999.** Table 2 gives **136/240** symptomatic tests, **206/240** with ultrasound-detected bubbles and **107/240** with symptoms severe enough to prevent completion. A 58-run subgroup with 17 DCS outcomes closely matches the upright arm in Katuntsev's results. The totals are held for overlap, and inconsistent arm percentages are excluded from extraction pending the original table image. The résumé transcription and archive files carry the provenance for both Russian sources. [Author summary](https://medical-diss.com/medicina/fiziologicheskiy-analiz-perenosimosti-chelovekom-vysotnoy-dekompressii)
- **Goodman and Cannon–Gould, 1964.** Goodman reports eight previously unpublished cases alongside six previously published cases. The eight were entered from Goodman. Four Royal Navy cases were entered from Cannon–Gould, including one previously reported by Coburn. These four are also Goodman's Cases 10–13 and are counted once. The selection results in Cannon–Gould include **18/365** people in source category C. The 365-person margin and its other categories are held for likely overlap with the broader Kenyon archive and Gould's earlier paper. [Goodman Table I](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/aeme/35/12/article-p1204.pdf) · [Cannon and Gould pp. 278–282](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1813311/) · [Kenyon Tables I–III](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19730007339/downloads/19730007339.pdf)
- **Malette et al., 1962.** The journal paper resolves a 1961 technical-report citation. The authors reviewed 35 previously reported dysbarism cases, 25 after chamber runs and 10 in aircraft. Those counts appear in the source register for provenance. [Original review](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/aeme/33/9/article-p1132.pdf)
