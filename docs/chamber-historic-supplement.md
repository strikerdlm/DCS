# Historical chamber trials and Royal Navy selection courses, release 1.15

Release 1.15 adds 57 chamber outcome rows and 19 Royal Navy course summary rows. The registry records 88 retrieval results across 15 operations, including Tavily searches and extracts, Firecrawl searches and PDF extraction, and direct archive checks. [Sources](../data/chamber/v1.15/sources.csv) · [Outcomes](../data/chamber/v1.15/observations.csv) · [Courses](../data/chamber/v1.15/selection_courses.csv) · [Overlap](../data/chamber/v1.15/overlap.csv) · [Search runs](../data/chamber/v1.15/search_runs.csv) · [Search hits](../data/chamber/v1.15/search_hits.csv)

| Source | Observations and scope |
| --- | --- |
| Royal Navy High Altitude Selection Test, Kenyon et al. (1972) | **898/2,565 courses** led to restrictions, including 764 Category B and 134 Category C outcomes. There were 2,135 people. A course comprised up to three planned runs to 37,000 ft. The year and role breakdowns are two partitions of the same courses. The 1957 denominator of 174 agrees with Table II and the three category cells in Table III, whose printed total says 177. The report describes an individual magnetic-tape file. Its download contains aggregate tables and coding documentation. [NASA report, Tables I–III and §III.B](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19730007339/downloads/19730007339.pdf) |
| Soviet chamber research, Genin (1980 translation) | Standard exercise with no tabulated preliminary desaturation yielded **0/17 at 6,000 m, 10/31 at 6,500 m, 83/173 at 7,000 m and 73/138 at 8,100 m**. Vakar's separately described series yielded **2/434 at 0.40 atm and 8/49 at 0.35 atm**, with a different exercise protocol. The database also records the suited series and separate person margins. [Table 1](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19800013491/downloads/19800013491.pdf#page=8) · [Vakar results](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19800013491/downloads/19800013491.pdf#page=9) · [Table 2](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19800013491/downloads/19800013491.pdf#page=10) |
| ACEL rapid decompression trials, Damato et al. (1965) | Eight main protocols each contribute 12 exposures. At 35,000 ft the two-hour oxygen prebreathe and 40-second ascent group recorded **10/12 bends**, while three hours of prebreathe with the same ascent time recorded **1/12**. Forced descents and the susceptibility standardization subset are flagged where they share exposures with other rows. [NASA Appendix A, Tables 2–3](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19650004850/downloads/19650004850.pdf#page=42) |
| Swedish rapid decompression, Balldin (1978) | **0/5 DCS symptoms** at each of 17,500 and 20,000 m. Doppler detected **0/5** and **1/5**, respectively. Three of the seven participants took part in both arms. The VGE detection occurred during descent. [Original Table II](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/asem/49/4/article-p599.pdf#page=3) |

The Genin scan gives two experiments, 14 people and 14 cases in Table 1 row 7. The exposure denominator is blank and the separately printed person tally is flagged. Table 2 group 3 lists ten experiments and 25 people. Its reported 0/10 is held. Groups 9 and 10 are concluding phases of earlier groups, so the database flags them for overlap. [Original scan](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19800013491/downloads/19800013491.pdf#page=8)
