# Archive follow-up and simulated zoom trials, release 1.23

Release 1.23 adds 10 observations and four source records, upgrades two existing sources, and updates one earlier cohort link. Seven new links record possible shared cohorts. The search ledger contains 27 operations and 94 screened results or retrieval attempts. [Observations](../data/chamber/v1.23/observations.csv) · [Source records](../data/chamber/v1.23/sources.csv) · [Source upgrades](../data/chamber/v1.23/schema.sql) · [Cohort links](../data/chamber/v1.23/overlap.csv) · [Search ledger](../data/chamber/v1.23/search_runs.csv) · [Screened results](../data/chamber/v1.23/search_hits.csv)

- **Berry and King, 1959.** The original report follows five survivors, three after chamber incidents and two after aircraft incidents. It also describes a separate June 1943 chamber episode in Case 2. The two episodes in that pilot are explicitly linked. The aircraft rows use peak cabin pressure altitudes of **29,000 and 31,000 ft**, as Table 2 specifies. These selected cases carry no incidence denominators. The citation relationship to Malette's literature review is retained with case membership unresolved. [Original Table 2](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.71422/page/n15/mode/1up) · [Case reports and discussion](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.71422/page/n5/mode/1up)
- **Allen and Beard, 1969.** The archived author abstract reports **322 chamber flights in 44 men**. Twenty men never reported bends, paresthesia or chokes, and the authors describe the other 24 as susceptible. The reported resting-flight counts of **1/10**, **12/46** and **28/79** are confined to those 24 men. The rows are held from independent pooling because the subgroup was defined by observed symptoms and participant overlap with earlier USAFSAM studies remains unresolved. The page image confirms the middle profile is **zoom 3000**. The full tables and all-flight outcome denominator by profile remain unavailable. [Author abstract A69-41292, bibliography p. 27](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19700011380/downloads/19700011380.pdf#page=34)
- **Remaining source leads.** The Berry–Wayne abstract describes approximately 130 hospitalizing Grade IV reactions in 1950–1955. The numerator is approximate and the underlying report, SAM 58-85, is still a retrieval lead. The recent Connolly source supplied the same abstract already indexed in v1.2. The publisher PDF returned HTTP 401. The exercise manuscript and RAND report provided no verified additional outcome rows in this pass. [Berry–Wayne meeting abstract](https://spacemedicineassociation.org/download/history/history_files_1957/Abstracts%201957.pdf) · [Connolly abstract](https://doi.org/10.3357/AMHP.6973.2026) · [Gottschalk author manuscript](https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:1909222/FULLTEXT01.pdf) · [RAND record](https://www.rand.org/pubs/reports/R1002.html)
