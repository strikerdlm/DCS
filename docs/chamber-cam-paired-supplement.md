# Paired chamber outcomes in the January 1943 CAM minutes

The v1.60 release adds three sets of evidence from the original sixth meeting of the Subcommittee on Decompression-Sickness. Source-defined symptom outcomes, forced descents and classifications stay in the evidence tables on overlap hold. [NLM meeting and table images](https://digirepo.nlm.nih.gov/ext/mm/101085500X233/PDF/101085500X233.pdf#page=135)

- **Ferris paired study.** The same 24 people contribute the placebo and stimulant conditions at 35,000 ft. The 24 cumulative counts cover two severity thresholds at six times. At 180 minutes the two conditions each report 24 people with bends or chokes. The incapacitating category contains 21 and 20 people, respectively. The original tables provide no paired response cross-classification or individual event times. The 1951 monograph independently links the study to Ryder et al., CAM Report 112, based on sample, altitude and exercise schedule. The meeting tables carry the outcomes once. [Meeting tables](https://digirepo.nlm.nih.gov/ext/mm/101085500X233/PDF/101085500X233.pdf#page=138) · [Fulton p. 238 and bibliography](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.116475)
- **Ivy's 40,000-ft series.** The three comparison groups have 105, 50 and 50 participants. The report gives percentages only and uses a symptom-tolerance criterion for forced descent. The 105-person placebo group shares a nominal altitude, dwell and sample size with Berkson's Pensacola series. The published descent metrics differ and participant linkage is unavailable. [Ivy report](https://digirepo.nlm.nih.gov/ext/mm/101085500X233/PDF/101085500X233.pdf#page=137) · [Pensacola table](https://digirepo.nlm.nih.gov/ext/mm/101085500X233/PDF/101085500X233.pdf#page=143)
- **Cincinnati and Canadian classifications.** The breadth-factor chokes table contains 6/17 and 15/18. A separate bends table places 10/13 and 5/17 in the more susceptible half of its sample. This is a rank-based outcome. Stewart reports 32/32 same-category retests, with potential overlap with the existing Halifax records. The Canadian time-of-day percentages lack denominators for each group. [Original page](https://digirepo.nlm.nih.gov/ext/mm/101085500X233/PDF/101085500X233.pdf#page=145)

The source register also links Larrabee's CAM 137, Pensacola's CAM 88 and Wright Field's CAM 99 to the relevant historical profiles. Report 137's title corroborates the Pennsylvania table's 38,000-ft heading while the meeting prose still gives 35,000 ft. The standalone report and participant linkage are unavailable. The 1947 Burkhardt et al. paper now has one DOI-based identity for four earlier search records. Fulton's similarly titled 1944 article remains a distinct report. [Monograph bibliography](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.116475) · [Burkhardt et al.](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.1947.02880060015004) · [Fulton](https://doi.org/10.1093/milmed/94.4.199)

For the existing 610-person RAF series, the catalogue range of AIR 57/22 covers the two known FPRC report numbers. The bundle is undigitized and the individual items have not been inspected. Robie et al. corroborate the Morant citation and describe three separate operational flight cases. Their historical summary cites Masland for the training fatalities and does not establish an independent chamber outcome. [Archive record](https://discovery.nationalarchives.gov.uk/details/r/C2151735) · [Robie bibliography](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/aeme/31/11/article-p885.pdf#page=12)
