# Historical age and weight-to-height strata

The v1.61 release adds published group data for severe symptoms during historical altitude chamber tests. The body-weight-to-height measure is the source's historical measure. Gray's relative susceptibility is a normalized group statistic. The new records remain on overlap hold and the clinical prediction benchmark retains its original evidence. [Gray chapter, Tables 40–41](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.116475)

- **Age.** The 29-row comparison captures 23 age strata and six printed programme totals. Gray reports 52,313 subjects across the six programmes. The 1995 Sulaiman reprint supplies the complete descriptors and clearly printed values. Readable original counts and rates stay alongside the reprint, with blank fields for unreadable cells. Pensacola's total is 78/946 in Gray and 88/946 in Sulaiman. Both print 8.25%, and both give 38/179 and 15.50% for the oldest group. The three counts sum to 88. The underlying case ledger is unavailable. [Sulaiman Table 2](https://archive.org/download/DTIC_ADA297023/DTIC_ADA297023.pdf#page=8)
- **Weight-to-height.** Gray Table 41 supplies 15 strata and three totals, including 99/2,217 at Kingman Field and 575/2,312 at Las Vegas. The Las Vegas flight heading has a degraded altitude digit. The altitude is blank pending verification. The table's rates and normalized susceptibility are retained as published. [Gray Table 41 image](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.116475)
- **Maxwell overlap.** Motley reports 11 weight-to-height strata in pounds per inch. Their denominators regroup exactly to Gray's five counts of 2,054, 12,695, 16,514, 10,417 and 2,517. Both tables refer to 44,197 people with weight-to-height data from the same cohort. The age subset has 44,172. Motley supplies separate rounded rates by symptom grade. Gray supplies exact severe symptom group totals. The database links the fine strata to the reanalysis and retains each publication's endpoint. [Motley Table IX](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/joam/16/4/article-p210.pdf#page=10)

`publication_gray_age_comparison` keeps the versions of each age row together. `publication_gray_density` holds the printed counts and `publication_motley_density` holds the rounded percentages and a link to each corresponding Maxwell row. The source register maps the original Gray chapter back to the existing canonical report.
