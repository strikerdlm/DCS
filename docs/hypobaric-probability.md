# Cumulative symptomatic DCS at a constant pressure plateau

Open **Hypobaric DCS** under **Model Families**, or send a scenario to `POST /api/v2/hypobaric/probability`. The response names the selected regression, the elapsed time and its group probability in percent. A valid request outside the supported profiles returns a null probability and a reason. Malformed values return HTTP 422.

This tool implements the follow-up guidance in NASA/TP-2020-220529, section 5.1 and Table 5. It uses regression coefficients estimated from symptomatic events and censored human chamber exposures. The ADRAC grid explorer, 4.3-psia exercise-prebreathe demo, and EVA telemetry simulator have separate input and endpoint definitions. [NASA full report](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20200004317/downloads/20200004317.pdf)

| Pressure | Source and endpoint | Implemented subset |
|---|---|---|
| 4.0–5.0 psia | 2020 Eq. 4, 125 symptomatic events in 1,031 exposures across 49 tests | 2–6 hours, arrival tissue ratio 0.92–2.02, BMI 18–30, known ambulation and repressurization history. The 10.2 → 14.7 psia REP protocol applies before a final exposure at 4.3 psia. |
| Above 5.0 through 7.5 psia | 1996 modified tissue-ratio regression, reprinted as 2020 Eq. 7, 211 cases in 1,075 male exposures across 66 tests | 2–4 hours, arrival tissue ratio 1.23–1.78, explicit male reference population, known ambulation and no staged repressurization. |

The report describes near-square decompressions over 5–30 minutes to constant pressure. Its discussion explicitly limits Eq. 4 to around 4–5 psia and recommends the pressure-dependent Eq. 7 above 5 psia. The software restricts the second branch to pressures demonstrated in Table 5 and omits the six-hour extrapolation identified in that table. The TR and BMI guardrails are conservative implementation choices based on examples in the report, including Table 2, Table 5 and Figure 6. Evidence for every combination within these ranges has not been established. The two independently fitted equations can disagree at the pressure boundary. [Original 66-test study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8834946/)

**Arrival tissue nitrogen** is the modeled nitrogen tension in psia at the start of the plateau, derived from the source-compatible 360-minute compartment and documented breathing and pressure history, including the ascent. Hold that value fixed when changing elapsed plateau time. Enter absolute ambient pressure in psia and elapsed time in hours starting at arrival. Ambulation means exercise included walking. An oximeter reading, instantaneous ambient nitrogen fraction, heart rate or isolated accelerometer reading cannot provide these declarations.

For a reproducible example, use 4.3 psia, tissue nitrogen of 5.289 psia (TR 1.23), a 15-minute transition, six hours of plateau exposure, BMI 24, ambulation and no repressurization. Eq. 4 gives **3.055%**, which rounds to the report's **3.1%**. The same scenario at four hours rounds to **1.8%**. Table 5's rounded tissue ratios give approximately 3% at four hours using Eq. 7. No individual uncertainty interval is supplied. The historical samples, reporting behavior and limited exposure protocols constrain transport to current crews and changing cabin pressure.

## Implementing ADRAC on one wearable page

Use one page for the observed pressure profile, exposure phase, confirmed oxygen and workload inputs, elapsed time, model identity and availability. Keep the atmospheric gas at altitude separate from oxygen prebreathe. Track the duration of a stable plateau, withhold estimates after a missing pressure history and present a hypothetical workload range only if all shown scenarios are supported.

Full original AFRL ADRAC includes a calibrated bubble-growth solver that supplies the time of maximum bubble radius to a stratified survival model. Re-fitting a surface to published grid outputs reproduces a different quantity. To claim full equivalence, obtain a versioned original solver or an independently reproducible specification including parameter units, boundaries, fitted strata and reference trajectories. Compare cumulative curves and endpoint predictions with the original program across pressures, gas histories and all exercise classes, then validate clinical accuracy on independent symptomatic exposures. Preserve the study's oxygen and workload conditions when interpreting a result.
