# Chamber Doppler and operational flight surveillance, release 1.22

Release 1.22 adds 11 source records, 23 observation rows and 10 source links. The search ledger records 24 operations and 72 screened results or retrieval attempts. [Sources](../data/chamber/v1.22/sources.csv) · [Observations](../data/chamber/v1.22/observations.csv) · [Cohort links](../data/chamber/v1.22/overlap.csv) · [Search ledger](../data/chamber/v1.22/search_runs.csv) · [Screened results](../data/chamber/v1.22/search_hits.csv)

- **Donnell and Norton, 1960.** The original describes the 43,000-ft chamber case that Goodman republished as Case 8. It gives approximately one minute at peak altitude and first symptoms during descent at 35,000 ft. Goodman lists two minutes at peak. Fourteen other trainees were present, and their clinical follow-up was unavailable. The index case is recorded once as a case report, with no outcome denominator. [Original pp. 1004–1006](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/aeme/31/12/article-p1004.pdf)
- **Berry and Smith, 1962.** USAF surveillance recorded **23 in-flight evolved-gas dysbarism reports in 1960** and **39 in January–September 1961**. The case-conditional tables cover 61 reports with symptom information and 60 with reported cabin altitude. Thirteen of 61 report neurocirculatory collapse. One cabin-altitude report below 20,000 ft is explicitly questioned by the authors. The number of flights at risk is unavailable. Berry and Smith's Case 3 matches Goodman's Case 9 and is linked through the surveillance source. [Original Tables I–IV](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/aeme/33/8/article-p995.pdf#page=4)
- **Filipenkov, 2015.** The conference report describes 447 chamber tests in 123 men, with 42 men affected and pulmonary manifestations in **4/42**. The author gives two diagnostic-value fractions, **77/259 tests** and **39/71 people**. The denominators refer to different units and subsets. Participant and protocol overlap with earlier Russian research is unresolved, so the pooled outcomes remain held from independent analyses. The author reports seven technically inadequate Doppler examinations at the participant level. [Author report](https://biofizpribor.ru/science/yubilejnaya-konferenciya-2015/ultrazvukovaya-diagnostika-ustoichivosti-k-dekompressii-pri-imitatsii-letnoi-deyatelnosti) · [Earlier patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/RU2387379C1/ru)
- **Katuntsev et al., 2016.** The Russian ISS operational record reports **zero clinical DCS signs in 96 person-EVAs** from June 2001 to August 2015. The same exposures are nested in the source's cumulative 287 person-EVAs. The ISS and cumulative rows preserve their original denominators and overlap. Their operational context differs from chamber research. [Full text and Table 1](https://cyberleninka.ru/article/n/rossiyskiy-opyt-meditsinskogo-obespecheniya-vnekorabelnoy-deyatelnosti-kosmonavtov-provedennoy-s-borta-mezhdunarodnoy-kosmicheskoy/pdf)

Tavily recovered both historical papers after the correct publisher issue was identified. Firecrawl extracted the original-language chamber and ISS reports. The verified PDFs and author HTML supply the source digests. Earlier Berry reports are retained as retrieval leads. [Input digests](../data/chamber/v1.22/manifest.json)
