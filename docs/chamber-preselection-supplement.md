# Henry chamber preselection tables

Henry’s 1947 paper reports two Berkeley chamber experiments that used pain scores from an initial chamber flight to rank susceptibility. The supplement transcribes 129 percentages from Tables I, III and IV into `publication_preselection_rate`. The source treats moderate or severe symptoms as a score of three or greater on the California scale. The table percentages retain the printed precision and source-defined endpoints. [Journal article, pp. 259–265](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/joam/18/3/article-p259.pdf) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20251251/)

In the first experiment, 53 men took the 38,000-ft classification test. Then 34 completed exercise validation II, 33 completed exercise validation V, and 13 took both. The 67 mixed validation exposures reuse the two exercise arms and share participants. Only the unselected rows in Table I carry exposure denominators in the database. Lower selection thresholds are nested subsets. Exact case counts were not available from the printed table. [Methods and Table I, pp. 259–260](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/joam/18/3/article-p259.pdf#page=2)

In the second experiment, 151 men took a Series E classification flight at 33,750 ft. The paper reports 53 men with two preoxygenated Series DN validation ascents at 35,000 ft and 98 men with a second Series E ascent. The exact denominators behind individual percentages in Tables III and IV were unavailable. The 27 unpreoxygenated controls in Table III are calculated values based on another study and an equal-correlation assumption. The other 102 entries across the three tables are reported validation rates. Table IV pools validation profiles with different target altitudes and prebreathe conditions. [Tables III and IV, pp. 262–263](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/joam/18/3/article-p259.pdf#page=4)

All rows remain on overlap hold. The 1947 report cites Henry’s 1946 study and repeats the Series E and DN procedures. The sample sizes differ and no participant-level crosswalk was recovered. The earlier Berkeley records remain in the database with their original scope. [Earlier Berkeley series](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21018673/)
