# Rapid-decompression chamber evidence, release 1.20

Release 1.20 adds one Canadian chamber series with one source-attributed probable neurological DCS case and an overlapping tally from the same paper. The source registry adds ten publications or archival leads. The audit includes six cohort links, 20 search or retrieval operations, and 94 logged hits. [Sources](../data/chamber/v1.20/sources.csv) · [Extracted observations](../data/chamber/v1.20/observations.csv) · [Cohort links](../data/chamber/v1.20/overlap.csv) · [Search operations](../data/chamber/v1.20/search_runs.csv) · [Screened hits](../data/chamber/v1.20/search_hits.csv)

- **Bryan, Leach and Stubbs, 1961.** Results p. 33 describes one case in 101 people exposed in Series 2, from 8,000 to 35,000 ft. Symptoms began during descent and the person had a seizure eight hours later. The authors presumed DCS with cerebral embolism. Their Discussion gives a broader tally of one case in 265 decompressions and refers to the same event. The source does not enumerate the additional runs or provide a cohort-wide follow-up window. The broader tally is flagged as an overlapping margin. Archdekin et al. is cross-referenced as a detailed report of that case. [Original Results and Discussion](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/aeme/32/1/article-p30.pdf#page=4)
- **Hoffler et al., 1974, and Bryan and Leach, 1960.** Each source describes 80 observations for oxygen performance or hypoxia. Hoffler's 80 are people and Bryan and Leach's 80 are chamber runs in eight people. Hoffler's DCS passage cites the Bryan 1961 cohort. A DCS count was unavailable for either hypoxia study. [Hoffler full text](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/aeme/45/2/article-p117.pdf) · [Bryan and Leach full text](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/aeme/31/4/article-p267.pdf)
- **Hale et al., 1968.** The paper gives 15/26 with pain in profile 2A and 7/13 in profile 1A, including three who required immediate recompression in 1A. The 13 were returning participants from the 2A subgroup. The authors describe their biochemical observations as a sub-study within the already indexed Degner flight program. The source and its technical-report counterpart are linked to the original cohort without adding observation rows. [Hale pp. 1171–1174](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/aeme/39/11/article-p1171.pdf) · [Degner et al.](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/aeme/36/5/article-p418.pdf)

Tavily recovered publisher full text. Firecrawl contributed search results, but its PDF scrapes returned only watermark text. Hall's two PubMed records resolve to one Naval School of Aviation Medicine report. The Hall and LeMessurier originals and the standalone Archdekin case presentation remain retrieval leads.
