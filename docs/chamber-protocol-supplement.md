# Pressure-suit and crossover chamber observations

Release 1.9 adds 23 aggregate observations from six primary papers and links two additional technical sources. The source register records exact pages and tables, plus SHA-256 hashes for the inspected PDFs. The Tavily and Firecrawl search log preserves 67 results and retrieval attempts across 11 operations, including requests that failed and were resolved through the publisher or a public archive. [Observations](../data/chamber/v1.9/observations.csv) · [Source registry](../data/chamber/v1.9/sources.csv) · [Search runs](../data/chamber/v1.9/search_runs.csv) · [Screening log](../data/chamber/v1.9/search_hits.csv)

| Primary source | Reported outcomes | Exposure and overlap notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Krutz and Dixon 1987 | Grade 2 bends in 12/14 exercise sessions and 8/14 flexion-only sessions. Grade 3–4 VGE in 10/14 and 7/14, respectively. | The same 14 men completed both arms at 30,000 ft after 60 minutes of oxygen prebreathe. The two arms are part of the Bends Screening Index project, which NEDU reports as larger groups. Individual VGE grades in Tables I and II disagree for one subject, so the database retains the published group counts. [Original paper](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/asem/58/9/article-pA97.pdf) |
| Dixon et al. 1986 and 1988 | DCS in 1/30 men and 5/30 women in separate repeated-exposure studies. Any VGE occurred in 22/30 men and 13/30 women. Two of the five affected women first reported symptoms the following day. Three received hyperbaric oxygen. | The cohorts match protocols in the NEDU 7.8 psia exposure groups. The female article counts affected people over the series, including delayed reports. NEDU reports 4/92 exposures. Individual linkage and the compilation’s counting rules are needed to reconcile the figures. Both primary series carry an overlap hold. [Male study](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/asem/57/3/article-p223.pdf) · [Female study](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/asem/59/12/article-p1146.pdf) |
| Webb et al. 1989 | Zero DCS and zero VGE among 21 people who completed the five-day 9.5 psia protocol. | Twenty-four started, and 21 completed the 8-hour daily exposures. Webb and Pilmanis 1993 reuses the 11 male completers in its 9.5 psia comparison. [Original paper](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/asem/60/5/article-p415.pdf) |
| Webb and Pilmanis 1993 | Grade 3–4 VGE in 0/10, 1/10, 2/10 and 4/10 exposures at 8.3, 7.8, 7.3 and 6.8 psia, respectively, during 100% oxygen use. Any VGE in 2/10, 2/10, 3/10 and 5/10. | The reported percentages and denominators yield exact integer counts. The matching NEDU DCS strata and the article’s historical comparators remain linked in the overlap register. [Table I](https://asma.kglmeridian.com/downloadpdf/view/journals/asem/64/9/article-p808.pdf) |
| Elia et al. 2021 | Zero DCS symptoms in each of the seated rest, squat and whole-body vibration conditions. Each condition had eight sessions. | The same eight Swedish volunteers completed all three 90-minute chamber sessions at 24,000 ft. Oxygen started 30–60 seconds before the first pressure drop. The symptom endpoint is limited to chamber observation. The article’s ultrasound summaries use the Eftedal–Brubakk scale. [Methods and Results](https://physoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1113/EP089522) |

All rows retain the source’s observation unit and outcome window. The experiment-level hold records and case-conditional rows are available for provenance and restricted contrasts. The database still uses the frozen development and external validation sets.
