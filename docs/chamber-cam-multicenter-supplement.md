# Wartime multicenter repeat-flight outcomes

The v1.59 supplement adds the Randolph Field preliminary summary and Berkson's 1943 analysis from the original NLM Bulletin. The database preserves historical chamber descents and symptom classifications. All outcome rows are on overlap hold because the original risk sets cannot be linked across reports and these endpoints can include causes other than clinical DCS. [Randolph primary summary](https://digirepo.nlm.nih.gov/ext/mm/101085500X233/PDF/101085500X233.pdf#page=74) · [Berkson primary tables](https://digirepo.nlm.nih.gov/ext/mm/101085500X233/PDF/101085500X233.pdf#page=140)

- The four-hour 35,000-ft Randolph series gives 145 symptom-driven descents among 380 subjects. Eight non-overlapping 30-minute bins partition the 145 descents. The separate printed one-hour 10% estimate likely uses the same flights since its denominator is also 380 and the first two bins total 38. Its numerator remains absent in the estimate table.
- In a comparison using the same 27 cadets, the two profiles each had 51 flights. There were 12 symptom-terminated flights at 35,000 ft and 24 at 38,000 ft. The 38,000-ft bends cell prints both 7 and 71% among the 24 symptom-terminated flights. Both values are retained and flagged. The 43-cadet five-flight series matches the design and size of a later Gray et al. group. The primary summary supplies flight-specific percentages and mean descent timing, while the later paper supplies the distribution of failures per cadet. [Gray et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20287795/)
- Berkson's 82 rows include flight margins and strata by prior descents at Randolph Field, the University of Pennsylvania, U.S. Navy Pensacola and Wright Field. A group with zero exposures retains the printed dashes as null outcome cells. The third-flight Randolph denominator of 66 with 3 descents and 50%, the Pensacola first-flight group percentage of 105 and the printed Wright Field percentage of 88.6 for 162 of 187 are preserved. The Pennsylvania table gives 38,000 ft. A nearby investigator summary describes 18 subjects and lists 35,000 ft. Both altitudes are available, with the discrepancy attached to the profile.
- The bibliography identifies CAM 330, 382 and 302 as distinct technical reports. CAM 330 names the exact 132 chokes among 329 subjects across 719 flights already extracted from the June minutes. The citation crosswalk links both reports to that evidence. CAM 382 and 302 are likely precursors to the existing Bridge journal reports. Full texts are needed to adjudicate participant and endpoint overlap. [Bibliography, references 3, 5 and 6](https://digirepo.nlm.nih.gov/ext/mm/101085500X233/PDF/101085500X233.pdf#page=424)

`publication_repeat_flight_profile` records the site, protocol, altitude and endpoint. `publication_repeat_flight_count` joins each profile to its original flight and history strata. `publication_descent_interval` joins the timing bins to a single parent count in `publication_observation`. `publication_reported_rate` retains estimates separately from reported counts.
