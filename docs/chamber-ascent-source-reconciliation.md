# Ascent compilation source reconciliation

## Air Force 9.5-psia validation

USAFSAM-TP-88-10 Study 5, VAX Title Code 21, reproduces the Dixon and Krutz conference abstract and specifies 20 men and 10 women, one exposure per person. The abstract reports zero bends in 30 exposure records, grade 1 or 2 bubbles in four of 20 men, and zero detected bubbles in ten women. It describes a six-hour simulation at 9.5 psia with 40% oxygen and no prebreathe. [Air Force report, printed p. 10](https://archive.org/download/DTIC_ADA201274/DTIC_ADA201274.pdf#page=14) · [Conference record](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19860063003)

NEDU Appendix E lists 12 men and 20 women for a protocol with the same pressure, duration, gas and activity description, with zero cases. The two sets of denominators are retained under an overlap flag. The Air Force report labels the experiment Aug–Nov 1986, after the December 1985 conference where the work was presented. The source date is recorded in the source note and omitted from the observation period pending reconciliation. [NEDU TR 18-01, Appendix E, p. E-1](https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/trecms/pdf/AD1101527.pdf)

The report separately lists six 9.5-psia exposures on 50% oxygen under its earlier Study 4. NASA's ascent appendix cites Dixon and Krutz for two rows with 30 and six exposures. The 30-exposure row links to the validation study. The six-exposure row may represent Study 4. Linkage across these studies remains uncertain at participant level. [Air Force Study 4, printed p. 9](https://archive.org/download/DTIC_ADA201274/DTIC_ADA201274.pdf#page=13) · [NASA Appendix B, printed p. 56](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19890012936/downloads/19890012936.pdf#page=62)

## Navy mixed-gas study

The Naval Aviation Safety Center newsletter reproduces the abstract of NAEC-ACEL-529. The scan shows 12 participants, 103 main ascents and 19 additional ascents. It gives four bends cases at the 18,000-ft equilibration altitude and one at 35,000 ft in the mixed-gas subgroup. Phase-specific denominators are unavailable. NASA Appendix B cites this report separately from the earlier Damato study in NASA TN D-2506, which has a different total and participant count. The new outcome margins are held for overlap review. [Navy newsletter, issue 10-65 p. 3](https://archive.org/details/FlightSurgeonsNewsletter1965/page/n150/mode/1up) · [Earlier NASA Damato report](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19650004850)
